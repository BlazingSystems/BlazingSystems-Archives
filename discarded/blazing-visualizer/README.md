# Blazing Visualizer / Avee work — DISCARDED

**Status: DISCARDED BY OWNER — 24 October 2026.** Development is stopped. This is an archive record, not an active project or a recommendation to continue.

## Decision

The owner chose to prioritize useful BlazeSystems products. This visualizer's mainly cosmetic value did not justify its development cost. Work expected to take roughly half a day to one day expanded well beyond that and consumed substantial time and Codex usage. The owner explicitly instructed that the idea be discarded and moved to the archive group.

Do not schedule further implementation, debugging, device checks, builds or releases. Reopening requires a new explicit instruction from the owner; the existence of a source snapshot or restart instructions in older documents is not authorization.

## Preserved history

The original Android experiment included local media playback, live note estimates, scene/preset editing, Practice and MP4 export. The owner reported that Preview 5 remained blank on their phone. Preview 6 passed synthetic emulator checks, but there was no owner-phone acceptance before the discard decision. Do not describe the phone issue as resolved.
